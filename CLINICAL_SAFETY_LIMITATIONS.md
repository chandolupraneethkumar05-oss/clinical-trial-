# ProtocolShield Clinical Safety, Algorithmic Boundaries & Decision Support Policy
**Document Classification:** Clinical Software Safety Specification  
**Version:** 3.0 Enterprise Clinical Decision Support Architecture  
**Regulatory Context:** 21 CFR Part 11 / FDA Modernized Oncology Trial Guidance / Clinical Decision Support (CDS) Boundaries

## 1. Clinical Decision Support (CDS) Statutory Disclaimer
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **MANDATORY INVESTIGATOR REVIEW REQUIREMENT:**  
> ProtocolShield is an **investigational decision-support simulation tool**, NOT a diagnostic medical device or autonomous patient qualification engine.  
> The screening results, composite burden indices, cohort attrition funnels, and amendment proposals generated by this software must be independently reviewed and confirmed by a qualified, board-certified clinical investigator prior to any patient enrollment, protocol alteration, or medical treatment decision.  
> This system does not constitute medical advice, medical diagnosis, or a formal binding trial eligibility determination.

---

## 2. Algorithmic Boundaries & Scope of Operation
ProtocolShield operates strictly within well-defined analytical boundaries:
1. **No Autonomous Treatment Recommendations:** The software evaluates historical and structured protocol eligibility criteria; it never prescribes, alters, or recommends therapeutic medical regimens.
2. **Deterministic Evaluation Engine:** Individual patient screening is evaluated deterministically against structured inclusion/exclusion rules and biomarker thresholds extracted from clinical trial protocols. No non-deterministic black-box LLM decision overrides clinical safety rules.
3. **Data Provenance Separation:** All patient data processed by ProtocolShield is classified by data provenance:
   - `synthetic_demo`: Statistically representative in-silico cohort records (e.g. 10,000 patient records modeling Stage III/IV NSCLC, TNBC, and Multiple Myeloma phenotypes).
   - `verified_clinical`: De-identified clinical records submitted with verified investigator provenance.

---

## 3. Four-State Deterministic Clinical Criterion Model
To prevent false-positive eligibility assumptions, ProtocolShield implements a rigorous 4-state evaluation model across all protocol criteria:

| State | Semantic Definition | Clinical Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `PASS` | Candidate's verified clinical observation fully satisfies protocol criterion threshold. | Criterion satisfied. |
| `FAIL` | Candidate's clinical observation directly breaches protocol inclusion/exclusion boundary. | Confirmed breach. If criterion is mandatory or a hard exclusion, results in `NOT_ELIGIBLE`. |
| `UNKNOWN` | Clinical observation is undocumented, unrecorded, or missing from the patient record. | Missing data. Evaluated as non-satisfied (`passed: false`). Must be resolved via lab draw or records retrieval. |
| `NEEDS_INVESTIGATOR_REVIEW` | Parameter is borderline (e.g. eGFR 50–59 mL/min under proposed amendment) or requires specialist pathology confirmation (e.g. brain MRI restaging, IHC TPS score). | Requires active investigator clinical judgment before qualification. |

## 4. Hard Exclusion Primacy Rule
> [!CAUTION]
> **NUMERICAL ELIGIBILITY SCORES CANNOT OVERRIDE MANDATORY CRITERIA:**  
> A candidate satisfying 13 out of 14 protocol criteria may achieve a mathematical criteria match score of **92.8%**. However, if the single failed criterion is a **mandatory inclusion requirement** (e.g. Stage IV requirement) or a **safety hard exclusion** (e.g. untreated active brain metastases or active systemic autoimmune disease requiring steroids), the overall trial status evaluates to **`NOT_ELIGIBLE` unconditionally**.

### Mathematical Outcome Resolution Rule:
1. If $\exists c \in \text{Criteria} : c.\text{is\_hard\_rule} = \text{True} \land c.\text{status} = \text{FAIL} \implies \mathbf{NOT\_ELIGIBLE}$.
2. If $\exists c \in \text{Criteria} : c.\text{is\_mandatory} = \text{True} \land c.\text{status} = \text{FAIL} \implies \mathbf{NOT\_ELIGIBLE}$.
3. If $\exists c \in \text{Criteria} : c.\text{status} \in \{\text{UNKNOWN}, \text{NEEDS\_INVESTIGATOR\_REVIEW}\} \implies \mathbf{REQUIRES\_REVIEW}$.
4. If $\forall c \in \text{Criteria} : c.\text{status} = \text{PASS} \implies \mathbf{POTENTIALLY\_ELIGIBLE}$.

## 5. Clinical Laboratory Cutoff Standardization & ULN References

### Alanine Aminotransferase (ALT) / Transaminase
- **Standard Clinical ULN Reference:** **`56.0 U/L`** (Adult Male / Mixed Reference Standard per clinical chemistry guidelines).
- **Protocol Ceiling Definition:** `max_alt = max_alt_multiplier * alt_uln_reference` (Default `2.5 x ULN = 140.0 U/L`) or explicit `max_alt_u_l`.
- **Consistency Enforcement:** The identical standard threshold is applied across both the DuckDB vectorized cohort funnel query and the individual patient screening engine.

### Hematologic & Organ Function Reference Limits:
- **eGFR (Renal Function):** Standard protocol threshold $\ge 60.0 \text{ mL/min/1.73m}^2$. Borderline candidates ($50.0 \le \text{eGFR} < 60.0$) evaluate to `NEEDS_INVESTIGATOR_REVIEW` under proposed amendment calibrations (AMD-01).
- **Absolute Neutrophil Count (ANC):** $\ge 1,500 \text{ cells/}\mu\text{L}$ to prevent severe neutropenia risks.
- **Platelet Count:** $\ge 100 \times 10^3 \text{/}\mu\text{L}$ for systemic cytotoxic and antibody-drug conjugate (ADC) safety.
- **Hemoglobin:** $\ge 9.0 \text{ g/dL}$ for adequate oxygen-carrying capacity during systemic therapy.
- **Total Bilirubin:** $\le 1.5 \text{ mg/dL}$ (hepatic clearance safety limit).

## 6. Central Nervous System (CNS) & Autoimmune Modernized Trial Guidance
- **Active / Untreated Brain Metastases:** Strict, non-overridable hard exclusion across all baseline oncologic protocols to prevent rapid intracranial progression complications.
- **Stable / Treated Brain Metastases:** In accordance with modernized FDA oncology clinical trial guidance (2020), protocols that accept asymptomatic, radiologically stable treated brain metastases (e.g. post-stereotactic radiosurgery) permit patient entry under specific protocol amendments (e.g. AMD-03).
- **Active Systemic Autoimmune Disease:** Hard exclusion for immunotherapy and checkpoint inhibitor trials due to high risk of life-threatening immune-related adverse events (irAEs).
